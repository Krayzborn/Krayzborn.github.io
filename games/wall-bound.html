Wall Bound: Pixel Dash

About the Game

Wall Bound: Pixel Dash is a pixel platformer auto-runner where one tap is all you need to reach the top! Dash, jump and climb your way to the finish with simple controls and tricky walls — every run tests your timing and patience.

Features

  • Simple One-Tap Controls: Easy to learn, hard to master
  • Checkpoint System: Saves your progress between game sessions
  • Hidden Alternate Paths: Discover secret routes to make the climb easier
  • Extra Lives: Watch optional ads to continue from your last checkpoint
  • Retro Pixel Art Style: Beautiful nostalgic visuals
  • No Account Needed: Just download and play
  • 100% Free: Perfect for quick sessions or long summit pushes
