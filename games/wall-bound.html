Wall Bound: Pixel Dash

About the Game

Wall Bound: Pixel Dash is a dynamic 2D platformer featuring unique wall jump mechanics and forgiving coyote time physics. Navigate through challenging levels by bouncing off walls and mastering precise platforming.

Features

  • Wall Jump System: Bounce off walls to reach new heights and explore hidden areas
  • Coyote Time: Forgiving physics that let you jump even after leaving a platform
  • Custom Character Controller: Smooth and responsive movement built from scratch
  • Pixel Art Style: Beautiful retro-inspired visuals
  • Challenging Levels: Test your skills with increasingly difficult platforming sections

Technical Details

  • Engine: Unity
  • Language: C#
  • Genre: 2D Platformer
  • Year: 2026
